---
title: Menus & Management
description: Open Inventory, Character, Journal, Party or World from one menu without leaving play. (C-527)
---

The play screen stays quiet by default: the scene keeps the screen, with compact
player/party status and the clock in the top corners and a single labeled
**Menu** entry beside them. The old permanent row of section buttons across the
top is gone.

## Opening management

Press **Menu** (or, once you have a section shortcut configured, use it
directly). Menu opens the management workspace on the section you used last —
Character the first time — and the section rail along the top lists all five:

- **Character** — your sheet: abilities, skills, traits and equipment.
- **Inventory** — what you are carrying and wearing.
- **Journal** — quests, your own notes, and session recaps.
- **Party** — your companions and what they bring.
- **World** — the codex: people, places, factions, lore and the gallery, plus
  faction reputation.

Switching sections takes one click. It replaces the section you were looking at
rather than opening a second window, so you always have a single workspace
instead of a stack of panels to close. Each section keeps its own state while
the workspace is open — a search you typed, a tab you picked, a note you started
are still there when you switch away and come back.

**Back** returns you to play, on the same spot in the world you left. The HUD
control you opened the workspace from gets focus back, so keyboard navigation
resumes where it left off.

## While management is open

The world pauses while the workspace is on screen, and the corner HUD widgets
(the clock, the autosave badge and the hotbar) step aside so the section rail has
the top of the screen to itself. Closing the workspace resumes the world.

Movement keys held through a transition are discarded: releasing a key after the
workspace closes will not make you walk. Press a movement key again to move.
