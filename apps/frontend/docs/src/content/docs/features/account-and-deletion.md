title: Your Account and Your Data
description: What your cloud account stores, how to sign out, and what happens when you delete your account or local data.
---

Your cloud account lets you back up your saves and restore them on another device. It is optional — the game is fully playable without one.

## What Lives Where

| Data | Device (Local) | Cloud (Account) |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| Campaigns, saves, chat history | ✅ Always | ✅ When you back up |
| AI provider connections | ✅ Always | ❌ |
| Character definitions, personas | ✅ Always | ❌ |
| Game settings, preferences | ✅ Always | ❌ |
| Account identity (name, email) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Published community packs | ❌ | ✅ |
| Cloud backups | ❌ | ✅ |

## Sign Out

Signing out removes your session from the current device. Your campaigns, saves, and chat history **remain on the device** — nothing is deleted. You can sign back in to the same account to resume cloud backups.

## Delete Local Data

**What it removes:**
- All local campaigns, saves, and chat history from this device
- All character definitions and personas
- All game settings and AI provider connections

**What it does NOT remove:**
- Your cloud account, identity, or backups (sign in again on another device and they are still there)
- Published community packs (they remain available to other players)

This action cannot be undone. If you have cloud backups, you can restore them after signing in on another device.

## Delete Your Cloud Account

**What it removes:**
- Your account identity (name, email)
- Your session on every device — you are signed out everywhere
- All cloud backups of your saves
- Your published community packs — they are transferred to a placeholder owner so other players can still use them

**What it does NOT remove:**
- Your on-device campaigns, saves, and chat history — those stay on your device until you delete local data
- Pack content (descriptions, images, asset metadata) — only the ownership is anonymised

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no grace period or undo.

> ⚠️ **If you want to keep your data**, back up your saves to the cloud first (Settings → Account → Cloud Sync), then delete your account. Your on-device data remains untouched.

## Compare: Account Delete vs Local Delete

| | Delete Cloud Account | Delete Local Data |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud identity removed | ✅ | ❌ |
| Cloud backups removed | ✅ | ❌ |
| Published packs transferred | ✅ | ❌ |
| Device campaigns removed | ❌ | ✅ |
| Device settings removed | ❌ | ✅ |
| Can be undone | ❌ | ❌ |
