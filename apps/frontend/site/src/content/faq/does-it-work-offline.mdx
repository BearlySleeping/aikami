---
question: "Does Aikami work offline?"
category: "gameplay"
order: 1
---

It can, once the required content is cached and a local text AI model is installed and
configured. Image and voice models are optional.

First-time content and model acquisition can require network access. Fully offline play
depends on keeping the content and local model available on your device.

Campaigns and saves are device-local. No account is required for local play or saves.

Cloud AI requires an internet connection and sends requests to the provider you choose.
Your saves remain local either way. See the [local setup guide](/tech) for model details.
