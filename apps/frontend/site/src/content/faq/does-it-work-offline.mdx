---
question: "Does Aikami work offline?"
category: "gameplay"
order: 1
---

Yes — after the first launch.

Aikami ships without its art library, which is what keeps the download small. The
first time you open it, the client fetches the sprites, maps, and audio it needs and
pins them in a local cache, verifying each file as it goes. You'll see that download
in the startup progress. **Every launch after that plays with no network connection
at all** — that includes the native desktop app.

Campaigns, saves, and chat history live in a local Turso (libSQL) database on your
machine and never require a connection. Signing in is optional and is never needed to
boot or play.

For a fully offline setup, run local AI too — the Docker stack (llama.cpp for text,
sd-server for images, sherpa-onnx/Kokoro for voice) means nothing leaves your machine
once the images and models are pulled. If you're using BYOK with a cloud provider
instead, you'll obviously need a connection to reach that provider, but your saves
stay local either way.

**Planning to play somewhere without a connection?** Launch it once beforehand. That
single successful startup is all it takes.
