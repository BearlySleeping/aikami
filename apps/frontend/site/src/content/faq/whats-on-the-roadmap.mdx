The current focus is a reliable authored adventure and player journey. Planned work then
connects persistent campaign time to physical resident routines, grounded town consequences,
reviewed campaign drafts to playable installations, and a complete community-package journey.

These efforts have dependencies and separate acceptance work; they are not release-date
promises. Follow the [project roadmap](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/issues) and
[campaign-generation work](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/issues/445).
