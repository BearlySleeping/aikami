Aikami’s code is [MIT licensed](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/blob/main/LICENSE).
Bundled assets have separate licenses. A text AI provider is required to play.

Local models use your hardware and may require you to download model files. Cloud AI
requires a provider account or key, internet access, and may incur charges from that
provider. Image and voice generation are optional.

No subscription is required by Aikami. See [asset licensing](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/blob/main/LICENSE-ASSETS.md)
before redistributing bundled artwork or audio.
