You can author structured content packs, and Aikami currently includes the authored
Emberwatch adventure. We’re also building toward campaign generation with reusable
structures such as inns, shops, camps, and roads, combined into new environments with
characters and quests. Generated assets would be optional.

Today, private narrative drafts can be generated and saved. They are not yet compiled or
installed as playable campaigns. That review and installation path is planned.

The Hub — where creators will browse, upload, remix, and publish packs the way people
share mods today — is still being built.
