---
question: "Can I make my own worlds and content?"
category: "gameplay"
order: 2
---

Yes. Every world in Aikami — maps, tilesets, sprites, props, NPCs, items, dialogue,
quests, encounters, factions — is defined in a structured **content pack**, not hardcoded
into the engine. Packs are plain files: copy one, change a line, and the world shifts.

The pack that ships with Aikami, *Emberwatch: The Fading Ward*, is a full village built
from a single manifest — an inn, a merchant, a persuasion-or-combat encounter, a faction
system, and a branching quest. Packs can be generated by AI or written by hand.

The Hub — where creators will browse, upload, remix, and publish packs the way people
share mods today — is still being built.
