question: "What is Aikami?"
category: "general"
order: 1
---

Aikami is an open-source platform for AI-powered 2D RPG adventures in early development.
We’re building toward a campaign creator that connects explorable places, memorable
characters, quests, and a world shaped by player choices.

Today, you can explore the authored Emberwatch adventure and save private narrative
campaign drafts. Turning those drafts into installed, playable campaigns is planned work.
You need a text AI provider; local saves do not require an account. Image and voice
generation are optional.
