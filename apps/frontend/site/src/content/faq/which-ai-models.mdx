All text, image, and voice generation flows through a single abstraction
(`AiProviderGateway`), so you can mix and match:

- **Text** — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
  Locally: llama.cpp by default, with Ollama or vLLM as opt-in swaps.
- **Images** — sd-server locally by default (ComfyUI is a supported opt-in swap); DALL·E,
  Stability AI, NovelAI, and fal.ai via BYOK.
- **Voice** — sherpa-onnx (Kokoro) locally by default; ElevenLabs, OpenAI TTS, VOICEVOX,
  and Fish Speech via BYOK.

A text engine is always required — that's the core of the game. Image and voice are
optional; the LPC sprite system covers the visual baseline with no AI dependency at all.
