---
question: "Where do my saves and chat history live?"
category: "privacy"
order: 1
---

On your machine. Campaigns, saves, and chat history are stored in a local database —
Turso (libSQL) on desktop, IndexedDB in the browser — and that local copy is the
source of truth for gameplay, not a server-side one.

There is a server side, but it holds very little. Cloudflare D1 stores account
identity and community content-pack metadata; Cloudflare R2 stores the public asset
catalog and, if you opt in, encrypted save backups. None of it is required to play:
your world boots, plays, and saves without an account.

Aikami downloads its art library on first launch, so that one startup needs a
connection — but that's public game art, not your data, and nothing about your
campaign is sent anywhere unless you deliberately sign in and turn on backups.
