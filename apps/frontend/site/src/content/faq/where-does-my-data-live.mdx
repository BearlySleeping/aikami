---
question: "Where do my saves and chat history live?"
category: "privacy"
order: 1
---

Primarily on your machine. Game data is stored locally by platform (Turso/libSQL for
desktop, IndexedDB for web), with the local database as the source of truth for gameplay.

Firebase is layered on top for auth and optional cloud sync/backup — if you sign in and
opt into sync, save copies go to Firebase Storage and some data (like NPC chat messages)
may be stored in Firestore for cross-device delivery. Your world plays and saves fine
without ever signing in; Firebase is not a boot dependency for gameplay availability,
though Firebase App and App Check initialize eagerly.
