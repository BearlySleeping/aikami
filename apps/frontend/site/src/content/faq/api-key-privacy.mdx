---
question: "Is my API key ever sent to Aikami's servers?"
category: "privacy"
order: 2
---

It depends on which mode you're using. When you use **BYOK for text generation**, your key
is stored in the client-side vault and requests are sent to the provider you configured.
That provider receives the request; do not assume cloud dialogue stays entirely on your
device.

With a configured **local text model**, text requests can run on your hardware. Offline
play also requires installed models and cached content. Optional image or voice providers
have their own network behavior.

If you'd rather verify than take our word for it, the client is
[open source](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami) — the provider gateway is all
right there.
