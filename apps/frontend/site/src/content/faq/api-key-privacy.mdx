---
question: "Is my API key ever sent to Aikami's servers?"
category: "privacy"
order: 2
---

It depends on which mode you're using. When you use **BYOK for text generation**, your key
is stored in the client-side vault and text requests go directly from your client to the
provider you configured (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, etc.). Aikami is not a proxy in that
path and never sees the key or the prompt.

With **local/offline mode**, nothing leaves your machine at all — llama.cpp, sd-server,
and sherpa-onnx/Kokoro (or Ollama/ComfyUI if you swap them in) run on your own hardware.

The **Service mode** (when available) routes requests through Aikami's infrastructure to
provide managed AI without requiring your own hardware or API keys. In that case, Aikami
telemetry receives routing metadata, though not your full conversation history.

If you'd rather verify than take our word for it, the client is
[open source](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami) — the provider gateway is all
right there.
