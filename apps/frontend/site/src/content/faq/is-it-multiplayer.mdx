---
question: "Is Aikami multiplayer?"
category: "gameplay"
order: 3
---

Not yet. Aikami is currently a single-player experience — you and the AI Game Master,
with an AI-driven party alongside you.

Your world can continue across your own devices if you sign in and opt into cloud sync:
play in the browser, pick up on the desktop app, same world, same NPCs. That's
multi-*device*, not multi-player — and sync is optional. Shared sessions with other people
are not in the current roadmap phases; if it's something you want,
[open a feature request](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/issues) — direction is
genuinely still up for discussion at this stage.
