---
question: "Is Aikami multiplayer?"
category: "gameplay"
order: 3
---

Not yet. Aikami is currently a single-player experience — you and the AI Game Master,
with an AI-driven party alongside you.

The same world state does stream across your own devices: play in the browser, pick up on
the desktop app, same world, same NPCs. That's multi-*device*, not multi-player. Shared
sessions with other people are not in the current roadmap phases; if it's something you
want, [open a feature request](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/issues) — direction
is genuinely still up for discussion at this stage.
