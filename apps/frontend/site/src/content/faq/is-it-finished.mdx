question: "Is Aikami finished? How stable is it?"
category: "status"
order: 1
---

No. Aikami is in early development, with a playable spatial foundation and an authored
Emberwatch adventure. Expect rough edges and incomplete player journeys.

Private narrative campaign drafts can be generated and saved. Persistent physical NPC
routines, grounded town-wide consequences, and compiling reviewed drafts into playable
campaigns remain planned work. Conversation recaps do not mean every resident knows what
happened or that its consequences have spread through the town.

If you hit a bug or want something to exist,
[open an issue](https://github.com/BearlySleeping/aikami/issues) or join
[Discord](https://discord.gg/XuuhWvSxHH).
